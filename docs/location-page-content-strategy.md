# PureBakes location-page content strategy

## Purpose and boundaries

The 41 audited URLs are retained as service-area pages with self-canonicals. PureBakes remains one premium custom-cake studio and pickup point in Manikonda. No page represents another branch, invents travel or delivery times, or claims local popularity, venues, demographics or verified delivery history.

## Differentiation model

Each location record carries an individually authored introduction, service context, three or four conservative nearby areas, three occasion links, two theme links, eight canonical gallery IDs and a four-question FAQ set. Shared HTML and verified payment/pickup policy remain centralized; customer-facing discovery content varies.

PROTECT pages retain legacy locality/custom-cake intent and Search Console evidence while receiving individually reviewed copy, stronger internal links and priority portfolio composition. IMPROVE pages receive the same authored-copy standard plus focused collection links. RETAIN pages are not token replacements: every introduction is authored separately and is paired with a distinct deterministic portfolio, FAQ mix and link set.

## Portfolio architecture

Location pages reference the one 472-record gallery. No location image folders, copies or duplicate records exist. A stable hash of the location key selects from the page’s occasion/theme emphasis and fills the remainder from the canonical portfolio; PROTECT pages begin with a rotating subset of approved featured records. The same data produces the same eight IDs unless gallery/configuration data changes. Captions and alt text are rendered unchanged.

The photography is described as design inspiration available to the locality. It is never labelled as delivered there without evidence. Responsive 360/720 WebP generation and lazy loading remain automatic.

## Nearby-area strategy

Nearby relationships are an explicit conservative adjacency map, not a rotating global list. Every target must be one of the 41 audited pages. Uncertain/non-audited areas are removed rather than used to create URLs. The Areas We Serve hub remains the complete discovery surface.

## Occasion, theme and FAQ strategy

Occasion links rotate among the existing birthday, reliable first-birthday split, anniversary, engagement, wedding and baby-shower pages. Theme links use only published floral, butterfly, jungle, princess, superhero, Spider-Man and unicorn pages.

FAQ content comes from a controlled verified pool. Every page has a locality-specific custom-cake question plus a varied combination covering pickup, assisted cab transport, lead time, eggless options, references or payment. Visible answers and FAQPage JSON-LD use the same data.

## Owner maintenance

Monthly gallery maintenance remains **one WebP + one JSON entry**. Location pages require no routine edits; a new record can become eligible through deterministic selection after the canonical gallery changes. New location URLs are not automatic and require a separate SEO/evidence review plus an explicit nearby-map entry and authored content.

## Audit artifacts

- `seo/location-content-differentiation.json`: hashes, image IDs, nearby areas, collection links and FAQ keys for all pages.
- `seo/location-content-validation.json`: similarity, overlap and fake-branch results.
- `seo/location-priority-review.json`: individual PROTECT/IMPROVE review evidence and preserved intent.
- `seo/location-migration-report.json`: legacy URL, classification, search evidence and internal-link inventory.
- `seo/location-copy-review.json`: internal V1 comparison, useful language retained, unsupported claims discarded and final customer copy.
